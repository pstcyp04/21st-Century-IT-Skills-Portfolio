## 🔹 About the Activity

This activity explores the importance of **prompt engineering** when interacting with Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools. It demonstrates how the quality and structure of instructions influence the relevance, accuracy, and usefulness of AI-generated responses.

Through this activity, I compared a simple and general prompt with a more detailed and specific prompt. The goal was to understand how providing context, clear instructions, and specific requirements can help AI generate outputs that better match the user's needs.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the concept and importance of prompt engineering.
- Recognize the difference between vague and specific prompts.
- Learn how context and clear instructions influence AI-generated responses.
- Develop prompts that communicate tasks and expectations effectively.
- Evaluate and improve AI-generated outputs through prompt refinement.
- Apply AI tools as learning and productivity assistants.

## 🧠 Prompt Comparison

The activity involved comparing two prompts for creating a study plan.

### 1. General Prompt

> **"Create me a study plan schedule."**

This prompt provides very limited information about the user's schedule, subjects, available time, and learning priorities.

### 2. Improved Prompt

> **Create a study plan schedule from 6:00 AM to 4:00 PM covering the following Quality Management System topics:**
>
> - Continuous Quality Improvement
> - Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award
> - Quality Tools and Problem Identification
>
> **Organize the topics into a structured schedule with appropriate study periods and breaks.**

The improved prompt provides specific topics, a defined time range, and clear expectations for the output. This allows the AI tool to generate a more organized and relevant study schedule.

## 📊 Key Differences

| Criteria | General Prompt | Improved Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Context** | Very limited | Provides specific subjects |
| **Instructions** | Broad and unclear | Clear and detailed |
| **Time Constraint** | Not specified | 6:00 AM–4:00 PM |
| **Expected Output** | General study schedule | Structured topic-based schedule |
| **Relevance** | May not match specific needs | More aligned with the intended task |

## ✨ Elements of an Effective Prompt

| Element | Importance |
|---|---|
| 🎯 **Clarity** | Makes the intended task understandable. |
| 📌 **Context** | Provides background information needed by the AI. |
| 📝 **Specificity** | Identifies the exact requirements and expected content. |
| ⏰ **Constraints** | Establishes limits such as time, format, or scope. |
| 📋 **Output Format** | Explains how the response should be organized. |

## 💡 Learning Reflection

Through this activity, I learned that the way we communicate with AI significantly affects the quality of its responses. A simple prompt may produce a general answer, but it may not always address the specific requirements of a task.

By improving the original prompt and including the topics, time constraints, and expected structure, I was able to provide clearer instructions and guide the AI toward a more useful response.

I also realized that using AI effectively requires more than simply asking questions. It involves critical thinking, identifying what information is needed, and refining instructions when the initial output does not meet expectations.

Overall, this activity helped me understand the value of prompt engineering as an essential digital skill. It showed me that AI can be a useful learning and productivity tool when guided by clear, specific, and well-structured instructions.
