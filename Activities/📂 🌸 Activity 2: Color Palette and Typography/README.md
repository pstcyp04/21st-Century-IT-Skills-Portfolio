## 📝 Learning Reflection

Through this activity, I gained a better understanding of how **color and typography work together to establish a recognizable brand identity**. I learned how to select complementary colors, choose appropriate fonts, and maintain visual consistency across different design materials.

One challenge I encountered was finding the right combination of colors and typefaces that would communicate an elegant and sophisticated floral brand without making the design look too plain. I addressed this by exploring different combinations and considering how each design element contributed to the overall concept.

In the future, I can apply these skills to branding projects, promotional materials, and other digital designs while improving my ability to create cohesive and visually appealing brand identities.
