## 💡 Concept

The infographic presents information about **Pax Silica** in a visually organized and easy-to-understand format. The goal was to transform a complex topic into **clear sections that audiences can quickly follow**.

## 📚 Main Sections

1. **What is Pax Silica?**
2. **What is its goal?**
3. **Expected economic benefits**
4. **Why Filipinos have concerns about it**
5. **The big question**

## 📝 Learning Reflection

Through this activity, I learned how to transform complex information into a visually engaging and understandable infographic. I developed skills in **information organization, visual hierarchy, content summarization, and visual communication**. One challenge I encountered was deciding which information was most important to include while keeping the infographic concise and avoiding overcrowded sections. I addressed this by organizing the content into five main sections and prioritizing the key points that would help readers understand the topic. In the future, I can apply these skills when creating educational materials, awareness campaigns, and social media content while continuing to improve my research, layout, and information-design skills.
